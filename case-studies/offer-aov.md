# Offer design: AOV nearly doubled to ~$125

**Role:** Founder and Growth Lead (Katz Ecommerce / LeadsUp)

## Context
Creative can only do so much on a weak offer. This case is about changing what the customer is offered.

## Problem
Average order value was too low to support paid acquisition at scale.

## What I did
- **Offer structure:** Reworked what is sold and how it is bundled and presented, so the default purchase was larger.
- **Creative:** Rebuilt the ads and page messaging around the new offer so the promise matched what was on sale.
- **Testing:** Compared the new offer against the old one on revenue per visitor and CAC, not just conversion rate.

## Result
- AOV nearly doubled, to about $125
