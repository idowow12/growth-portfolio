# D2C launch: EUR 19K in 2.5 days, TikTok-led

**Role:** Founder and Growth Lead (Katz Ecommerce / LeadsUp), built solo
**Channel:** TikTok-led

## Context
A direct-to-consumer product I launched myself, with no team and a deliberately small media budget.

## Problem
Prove that a product can go from zero to meaningful revenue fast, with creative doing the work that a large budget normally would.

## What I did
- **Creative strategy:** Built the launch around native TikTok-style content rather than polished brand ads, with several angles in market from the start.
- **Testing:** Launched multiple creatives at once, let the data pick the winners, and moved spend to them.
- **Offer and funnel:** Handled offer, page and ad creative myself so the whole loop stayed fast.

## Result
- EUR 19K in the first 2.5 days
- EUR 82K+ gross in total at about 40% margin
- Under $8K in media spend
