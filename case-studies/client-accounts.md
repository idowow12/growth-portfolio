# Client accounts up to $27K/month at ~2.5 ROAS

**Role:** Founder and Growth Lead (Katz Ecommerce / LeadsUp)
**Clients include:** Isrotel, Achim Limor

## Context
Paid social for client businesses, with budgets up to $27K/month on a single account.

## Problem
Clients need predictable returns, which means repeatable creative production and honest measurement.

## What I did
- **Creative strategy:** Built an offer-and-angle plan per client and produced ads against it in weekly batches. See [briefing 15+ ads a week](../process/briefing-15-ads-a-week.md).
- **Testing:** Used one testing framework across accounts so results were comparable. See [testing and kill rules](../process/testing-and-kill-rules.md).
- **Scaling:** Put budget behind proven winners and refreshed creative before fatigue showed up in results.

## Result
- Up to $27K/month in spend at about 2.5 ROAS

*Per-client numbers are private.*
