# Phone-sales desk: $165K in 3 months

**Role:** Founder and Growth Lead (Katz Ecommerce / LeadsUp)
**Team:** 10 sales agents

## Context
A lead-generation operation feeding a phone-sales team, where ad creative has to produce leads that the desk can close.

## Problem
Volume of leads is easy to buy. Leads that turn into paid orders at a cost that works is the actual job.

## What I did
- **Creative strategy:** Wrote ads and lead messaging to attract buyers, not browsers, and tested angles on lead quality as well as cost.
- **Lead flow:** Fed the 10-agent desk with 11,700+ leads at EUR 1.01 to $1.44 CPL.
- **Tooling:** Built lead-management and call-routing tools to run the desk.

## Result
- $165K in 3 months
- 11,700+ leads at EUR 1.01 to $1.44 CPL
- A 10-agent team running on the system
