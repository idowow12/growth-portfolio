**Role:** Founder, under Katz Ecommerce / LeadsUp
**Category:** Cosmetic oral care, Shopify

## Context
A cosmetic oral-care brand I built on Shopify as an owner, not as an agency.

Build a brand from nothing to the point where someone else wanted to buy it.
