# Saphira Hair: Meta at $30-40K/month, CAC cut ~50%

**Role:** Digital Marketing Associate and sole marketer, Sep 2023 to Dec 2024
**Brand:** Saphira Hair, a US consumer brand at $1M+/month revenue
**Channel:** Meta

## Context
A growing US hair brand where one person owned paid social, lifecycle and reporting. Meta spend ran at $30-40K/month for 16 months, about $500K in total.

## Problem
Acquisition cost was too high for the margin the brand wanted to scale at, and there was no CRM or email system to earn money back from existing customers.

## What I did
- **Creative strategy:** Treated creative as the main lever. Mapped the offer to distinct angles and awareness levels, and produced a steady flow of new ads against each angle instead of polishing a few.
- **Testing:** Ran angles as separate tests, judged ads on CAC rather than surface metrics, cut losers quickly and put budget behind winners. See [testing and kill rules](../process/testing-and-kill-rules.md).
- **Iteration:** Took winning ads and rebuilt them with new hooks, openings and formats. See [iterating winners](../process/iterating-winners.md).
- **Retention:** Built the CRM and email program from scratch, so more of each acquired customer's value came back through owned channels.

## Result
- CAC cut by roughly 50%
- $30-40K/month managed on Meta for 16 months
- CRM and email built from zero
- Experience scripting and briefing an ad that spent $100K+ in a single month
