# Example / template: hook bank

> **Example / template.** 20 sample hooks written for a hypothetical dog calming chew. They show how I organize hooks by angle and awareness level. They are not hooks from past client ads.

**Awareness levels:** Unaware (does not know there is a problem), Problem-aware (feels it, no solution), Solution-aware (knows options exist), Product-aware (knows us, needs a push).

## Angle 1: Social pressure
| # | Level | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem-aware | "My neighbor left a note about my dog barking." |
| 2 | Problem-aware | "I stopped inviting people over because of how my dog reacts." |
| 3 | Solution-aware | "I tried three calming products and my landlord still called." |
| 4 | Product-aware | "Everyone on my street keeps asking what changed with my dog." |

## Angle 2: Guilt and routine
| # | Level | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Unaware | "Watch what your dog does for the first ten minutes after you leave." |
| 6 | Problem-aware | "I used to dread the moment I picked up my keys." |
| 7 | Problem-aware | "I felt like a bad owner every time I left for work." |
| 8 | Product-aware | "Here is what my morning looks like now." |

## Angle 3: Skeptic converted
| # | Level | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | Solution-aware | "I did not believe a chew could help. Then I filmed this." |
| 10 | Solution-aware | "I only ordered it so I could write a bad review." |
| 11 | Solution-aware | "I have wasted money on every dog product, so I was careful with this one." |
| 12 | Product-aware | "Two weeks in, here is my honest take." |

## Angle 4: Education
| # | Level | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | Unaware | "These 3 signs mean your dog is stressed, not misbehaving." |
| 14 | Unaware | "Pacing, panting and chewing doors: here is what it usually means." |
| 15 | Solution-aware | "Why training alone did not fix my dog's anxiety." |
| 16 | Solution-aware | "What to look for in a calming chew before you buy one." |

## Angle 5: Comparison and offer
| # | Level | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | Solution-aware | "Calming chew vs calming spray: what I would pick." |
| 18 | Product-aware | "Is the starter bundle worth it? Here is what is inside." |
| 19 | Product-aware | "Still thinking about it? Here is what I wish I knew sooner." |
| 20 | Product-aware | "If you only try one thing this month, make it this." |

## How to use
Pick the angle, then the level that matches the audience temperature. Test hooks against the same body so the result isolates the hook. See [iterating winners](../process/iterating-winners.md).
