# Example / template: UGC creator brief

> **Example / template.** A hypothetical brief for an imaginary dog calming chew, showing the format I send to gifted creators. It is not a past client brief.

## Hi, and thank you!
We are sending you a free product. In return, we would like one video you film yourself. Real and casual beats polished.

## About the product
PawCalm is a calming chew for dogs that get anxious when left alone. (Hypothetical product.)

## What we need
- **Length:** 25-35 seconds
- **Format:** vertical 9:16, filmed on your phone, natural light
- **Deliver:** raw file, no music, no heavy edits, we will do the rest

## The story to tell
1. **Open with the problem.** Say what your dog does when you leave. Start in the first 3 seconds.
2. **Show it.** Film the dog, the home, the moment of leaving.
3. **Share your honest first reaction.** Were you skeptical? Say so.
4. **Show using the product.** Hold it, give it to your dog.
5. **Tell us what you noticed.** Only what is true for you.
6. **Finish with one line:** "Worth a try if your dog struggles when you leave."

## Hook options (pick one, or use your own)
- "My neighbor left a note about my dog barking."
- "I did not believe a chew could help."
- "Here is what my mornings look like now."

## Do
- Speak like you would to a friend
- Show your real dog and home
- Film a second take with a different opening

## Do not
- Make medical claims or say it treats or cures anything
- Promise results you have not seen
- Read from a script word for word

## Logistics
- Send within 10 days of receiving the product
- Reply with questions anytime
- If the video performs well, we may invite you to create more
