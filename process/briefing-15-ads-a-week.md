# Briefing 15+ ads a week

A weekly rhythm that keeps fresh creative flowing without chaos.

## The weekly cycle
- **Monday: read the results.** What won, what lost, what the data says about the customer.
- **Tuesday: set the plan.** Decide the angles for the week and how many ads go to each. About 15 ads split across new angles, hook swaps on winners and format variations.
- **Wednesday: write and brief.** One-page briefs per concept (see [ad brief template](../samples/ad-brief-template.md)), creator briefs for UGC (see [creator brief](../samples/ugc-creator-brief.md)), hooks drawn from the [hook bank](../samples/hook-bank.md).
- **Thursday: produce and review.** Creators deliver, AI variants are generated, everything is checked against the brief.
- **Friday: launch.** Ads go live in a clean test structure with clear naming.

## Mix for a 15-ad week
- Most slots: **new hooks on proven angles**, the highest-odds slot.
- Some slots: **new angles**, so the account keeps learning.
- A few slots: **format variations** of the current winners.

## What makes it work
- **Briefs are one page.** If it needs more, the idea is not clear.
- **Naming is strict:** angle, hook, creator, format, date. Without it, the results cannot be read.
- **Every ad has a hypothesis.** "Test this because the customer said X."
