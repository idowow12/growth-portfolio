# Testing and kill rules

The framework I apply to creative tests. Exact thresholds are set per account from its break-even CAC and margin, never copied from another account.

## Before launch
- Set the **break-even CAC** and the **target CAC** for the account.
- Test **one variable at a time** where possible: angle first, then hook, then format.
- Group ads by angle so results read as a lesson about the customer, not just a winner.

## Judging
- Scoreboard: **CAC and margin**, not clicks or cheap views.
- Use leading signals (hook rate, hold rate, CTR) to **diagnose**, not to declare a winner.
- Give each ad enough spend to be judged fairly before deciding. Define that spend as a multiple of target CAC.

## Kill rules (framework)
- **Kill** an ad that spends a set multiple of target CAC with no conversions.
- **Kill** an ad that is clearly above break-even CAC once it has enough data.
- **Pause, do not delete,** anything with a strong leading signal and weak conversion. The problem may be the landing page or offer.
- **Keep** ads that sit near target and iterate on them.

## Scale rules
- Winners get budget increases in steps, watching CAC as spend rises.
- Winners also get iterated (see [iterating winners](iterating-winners.md)), because every ad fatigues.

## Log
Every test is logged with the angle, the hypothesis and the result, so the next brief starts from evidence.
