# The UGC factory

A repeatable way to turn creators into a steady supply of ad creative.

## Numbers
- Creators recruited through **gifting**
- About **$18-20 per video**
- Run in **waves of hundreds of videos**
- **Scale the top 5-10%**

## The loop
1. **Define the angles first.** Pick the offers and angles worth testing, so creators are filmed against a plan. See the [creator brief example](../samples/ugc-creator-brief.md).
2. **Recruit through gifting.** Send product, brief the creator, collect the video. Low cost per video keeps the downside small.
3. **Run a wave.** Hundreds of videos is the point: with a low hit rate, volume is what produces winners.
4. **Test everything in market.** Every video is an ad test. Judge on CAC and margin.
5. **Cut to the top 5-10%.** Most videos will not earn more spend. That is expected and priced in.
6. **Scale and brief the winners.** Pay for more from the creators who delivered, give them sharper briefs based on what won, and feed the learnings into the next wave.

## Why it works
At $18-20 a video, a wave of hundreds costs far less than a studio shoot. The top few percent carry the account, so the system is built to find them fast and learn from them.
