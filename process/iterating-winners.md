# Iterating winners

A winning ad is a lead, not a finish line. The job is to find out why it worked and make more of it.

## Steps
1. **Name the reason.** Which element carried it: the hook, the angle, the creator, the offer, the proof?
2. **Change one thing at a time.**
   - **New hook, same body:** the cheapest and most useful iteration.
   - **New creator, same script:** tests whether the script carries it.
   - **New format, same idea:** UGC to static, short to long, 9:16 to 4:5.
   - **New awareness level:** same angle, retold for colder or warmer buyers.
3. **Use AI to widen the set.** Higgsfield, Kling and Nano Banana help produce variants, visuals and concepts faster. Human judgment picks what ships.
4. **Brief the next wave from the winner.** Feed the winning structure and language back into the [creator brief](../samples/ugc-creator-brief.md).
5. **Watch fatigue.** When CAC drifts up on a winner, the next iteration should already be in testing.

## Rule of thumb
Keep the **angle**, change the **packaging**. Packaging fatigues first.
